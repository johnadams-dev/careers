About the Foundation

More Than a Brokerage.
A Community Institution.

Adams, Cameron & Co. has been a fixture of Volusia and Flagler County life since 1963. The Adams Cameron Foundation is the formal expression of a commitment that has always been part of our DNA.

Every agent who joins our family becomes part of a broader mission: to strengthen the communities where our clients live, work, and raise their families. Through charitable giving, volunteerism, and community partnerships, the Foundation channels the collective resources of the Adams Cameron family toward causes that matter.

Our focus areas include education, housing assistance, veterans’ services, and local nonprofits that serve Volusia and Flagler County residents in need. We believe that a thriving community creates a thriving real estate market—and that great neighbors make great neighbors.

Focus Areas

Where We Invest.

Our giving is focused on the causes and communities that define Volusia and Flagler County.

Education & Youth

Supporting local schools, scholarship programs, and youth initiatives that give every young person in our region the foundation they deserve.

Housing & Stability

Partnering with organizations that address housing insecurity and help families in Volusia and Flagler County find stable, safe homes.

Veterans & First Responders

Honoring those who serve by supporting veterans’ organizations and first responder programs across our region.
