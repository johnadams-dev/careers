Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

Have a question not answered here? Call us at (386) 243-9504.

Do I have to meet any production minimums?

No. The referral program has no production minimums, no transaction requirements, and no sales quotas. You simply maintain your license with us and refer when the opportunity arises.

Are there desk fees or monthly costs?

No. There are no desk fees and no monthly costs. The only fee is a small $35 annual filing fee to maintain your license in the program. That’s it.

What happens when I want to return to active selling?

Because your license remains active, returning to full practice is straightforward. Simply notify us and we’ll transition you to an active agent plan with full access to our support ecosystem.

Can I refer clients outside of Volusia and Flagler County?

Yes. Through our membership in Leading Real Estate Companies of the World, we can place referrals with trusted partner brokerages across all of Florida, all 50 U.S. states, and 70+ countries worldwide.

How are referral fees paid and when?

Referral fees are paid at the closing of the referred transaction. The specific percentage is outlined in your referral agreement and is paid directly to you after settlement.
